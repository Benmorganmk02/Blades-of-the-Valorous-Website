WHO CAN TAKE THIS BACKGROUND — pick ONE of these two ways:

1. Simple: list the archetypes and races. A hero can take it only if BOTH match.
     archetypes: Ranger, Rogue
     races: all

2. Exact combinations: add a `combos` line (this replaces the two lines above).
   Separate combinations with commas, and join archetype and race with "+".
   Use `any` for "any archetype" or "any race".
     combos: Ranger + Canideran, Rogue + Felineran, Druid + any

Names don't care about capitals or accents (Dúrhennar = durhennar).
