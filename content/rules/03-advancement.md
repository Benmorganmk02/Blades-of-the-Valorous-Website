Characters start at level 0. Each level grants points to spend in four pools.

| Pool               | Points                                  |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Attribute points   | 6 + 6 per level                         |
| Skill points       | 12 + 8 per level                        |
| Ability points     | 2 + 1 per level                         |
| Proficiency points | 5, then +4 / +5 alternating each level  |

Raising a stat costs points along the **prime ladder**: each level costs the next prime. You may lower a stat below 0 to earn points back.

| Raise to | Cost of that level | Total spent |
| -------- | ------------------ | ----------- |
| +1       | 1                  | 1           |
| +2       | 2                  | 3           |
| +3       | 3                  | 6           |
| +4       | 5                  | 11          |
| +5       | 7                  | 18          |
| +6       | 11                 | 29          |
