---
title: Injuries
order: 7
---

Successes that get past a location's armor can cause an injury there. Injuries stack: two flesh wounds cost two dice.

| Injury        | Successes through armor | Effect                                                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flesh Wound   | 3                       | −1 die to attacks                                         |
| Minor Injury  | 5                       | −2 dice to attacks                                        |
| Maiming       | 6                       | −3 dice to attacks; a struck limb is unusable             |
| Dismemberment | 7                       | A struck limb is lost. A blow to the Head kills outright  |
| Instant Kill  | 8+                      | The character is slain                                    |
