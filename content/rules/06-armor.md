---
title: Armor
order: 6
---

Armor protects **eight slots**: head, torso, both shoulders, both arms and both legs. Each slot can carry four layers, and the layers stack. Shoulder armor guards both its arm and the torso.

| Layer        | Max points |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| Clothing     | 1          |
| Inner armor  | 2          |
| Middle armor | 3          |
| Outer armor  | 5          |

A blow that armor absorbs still wears it down by one point, starting with the outermost layer. **Hard armor** such as plate only degrades under a heavy attack or a dedicated armor-breaker.
