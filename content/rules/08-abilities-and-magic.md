Ability points unlock abilities from archetype trees. Higher tiers require the ones below them.

- **Open Combatant**: heavy-weapon brawlers, with areas of denial, terrifying finishers, and the reckless strength of Berzerk.
- **Ranger**: archers and skirmishers, with volleys of extra arrows, ricochets, and finding the flaw in a foe's guard.
- **Specialist**: *coming soon.*
- **Zealot**: *coming soon.*
- **Druid**: *coming soon.*

### Schools of Magic

Spellcasters learn from five schools. Bolts are the base spells, and modifiers reshape them with extra mana: blasts, chains, lingering effects and more.

| School              | Disciplines                              |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Elements & Arcane   | Fire, Lightning, Frost, Poison, Arcane   |
| The Mind            | Illusions, Mind Affects                  |
| Material            | Enhance Body, Craftsmanship              |
| The Zealot          | Holy, Faith                              |
| The Druid           | Nature, Beast                            |
