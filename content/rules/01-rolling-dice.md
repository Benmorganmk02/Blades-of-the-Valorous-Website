---
title: Rolling Dice
order: 1
---

Every test is a **pool of six-sided dice**. A skill roll uses **3 + Attribute + Skill** dice. Each die that meets the target number counts as one **success**. The more successes you roll, the better the outcome.

| Circumstance        | Success on |
| ------------------- | ---------- |
| Advantage           | 3+         |
| Normal              | 4+         |
| Disadvantage        | 5+         |
| Severe disadvantage | 6          |

Advantage and disadvantage cancel each other out. Stacked disadvantage pushes the target to 6.

**Damage:** roll the weapon's damage die and multiply it by your successes.
