title: Health & Resources
order: 4
---

| Resource   | Maximum                                                                    |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hit Points | 200 + 10 per level                                                         |
| Stamina    | 3 + STR + Endurance (minimum 3)                                            |
| Mana       | 3 + the better of INT + Advanced Knowledge or WIS + Intuition (minimum 3)  |
| Reactions  | 1 + (STR + DEX + WIS + INT) ÷ 8, rounded down, per round                   |

**Stamina** fuels martial abilities and **Mana** fuels spells. **Reactions** let you respond outside your turn: parry, dodge, or trigger an ability.
