---
title: Proficiencies
order: 5
---

Proficiencies represent training with a weapon, tool or vocation. They are bought with proficiency points on the same prime ladder.

- **Weapons** roll 3 + a physical attribute (STR or DEX) + a mental attribute (WIS or INT) + proficiency level.
- **Bows** aim with DEX, adding the bow's draw-weight bonus and your proficiency.
- **Crossbows** trade training for a flat 3 + INT + DEX attack.
