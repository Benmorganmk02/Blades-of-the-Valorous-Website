---
title: Attributes & Skills
order: 2
---

Six attributes define a character. Each one governs three skills.

| Attribute            | What it covers                    | Skills                                            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Strength** (STR)     | Raw power, endurance and grip     | Athletics, Endurance, Grip                        |
| **Dexterity** (DEX)    | Agility, balance and quiet feet   | Acrobatics, Stability, Stealth                    |
| **Wisdom** (WIS)       | Instinct, awareness and the wild  | Discernment, Intuition, Survival                  |
| **Intelligence** (INT) | Learning, lore and deduction      | General Knowledge, Advanced Knowledge, Investigation |
| **Cunning** (CNG)      | Guile, misdirection and wit       | Converse, Deception, Infiltration                 |
| **Charisma** (CHA)     | Presence, voice and influence     | Convey, Performance, Persuasion                   |
